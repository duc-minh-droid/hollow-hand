## Keywords and statuses

| Keyword | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Ward** | Blocks incoming damage. Clears at the start of your turn. |
| **Pierce** | Ignores Ward. |
| **Ash** | Burns away after use. Gone until the fight ends. |
| **Toll** | Pay HP instead of candles. |
| **Sigil** | Stays in play for the rest of the fight. |
| **Unplayable** | Cannot be played. |
| **Doubt** | A curse card. Unplayable. Clogs your hand. |
| **Reversed** | Drawn upside-down (20% chance). The reversed text applies — usually stronger, always with a price. |
| **Bleed** | Lose HP equal to Bleed at the start of your turn, then Bleed drops by 1. Ignores Ward. |
| **Hex** | Your attacks deal 25% less damage. Drops by 1 at end of your turn. |
| **Frail** | You gain 25% less Ward. Drops by 1 at end of your turn. |
| **Thorns** | When struck by an attack, the attacker loses that much HP. Fades at the start of your turn. |
| **Fury** | Each hit of your attacks deals that much extra damage. Lasts the whole fight. |
| **Omen** | Your next attack card deals double damage. |
| **Dread** | Lose that many candles at the start of your next turn. |
| **Veil** | The next attack that would hit you passes through harmlessly. |
| **Twice** | Your next card is played twice. |
